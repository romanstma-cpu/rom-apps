ROM PolyBot 2.36.1 adds a clear answer to “Why am I not seeing trades?” on the Overview page. It shows the first current obstacle, such as a paused strategy, disconnected Polymarket US API, unavailable buying power, missing qualified live evidence, a stopped market feed, or an old decision cycle. The action beside the explanation opens the relevant page.

The panel separates market trades and signals recorded over the last 24 hours from candidates and entries in the last confirmed scan. An entry created by the app is not presented as a filled order. Practice remains available with simulated funds when the live account has no buying power; live evidence, execution, and risk requirements are unchanged.

This update changes diagnostics only. It does not loosen trading filters, increase trade frequency, place an order during setup, or establish profitability. No live order was placed during validation.
