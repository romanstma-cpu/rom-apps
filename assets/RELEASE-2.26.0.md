# ROM Polybot 2.26.0

ROM Polybot 2.26.0 adds post-fill execution measurement and a conservative adverse-selection guard.

- Live main-strategy fills record side-aware movement after 30, 120, and 300 seconds.
- Comparable entries can pause only after 30 independent market-day observations across at least 10 days and 8 markets show clearly unfavorable movement.
- The Evidence screen displays guard collection progress and any active pause.

This release does not claim guaranteed or improved returns. The Windows installer is unsigned and the Apple Silicon installer is not Apple-notarized. Verify each download against its published SHA-256 file.
