ROM Polybot for macOS
====================

1. Drag ROM PolyBot into Applications.
2. Open it from Applications, then connect your Polymarket US API account.
3. Review your strategy and limits. Trading starts paused.

Choose Apple Silicon for an M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor.
Check Apple menu > About This Mac if you are unsure.

This community build is not Apple-notarized. If macOS reports an unidentified
developer, verify the download checksum and source before deciding to open it.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445

If macOS reports damage or malware, do not assume the warning is harmless.
Delete the download and contact ROM Apps support. Do not disable Gatekeeper
or remove security attributes to bypass that warning.
