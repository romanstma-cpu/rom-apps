Which app is right for me?

Choose Polybot for a Polymarket US trading workspace, Nova for Solana research, Trader for Kalshi strategy testing, or Scribe for transcripts and subtitles. Each app works independently.

Are the apps free?

The downloads listed here are free. Exchange trading fees, account requirements and any third-party services are separate. Check the connected service before using it.

What do I need for Polybot?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit), or a Mac running macOS 15 or later, plus a verified Polymarket US account, and its API Key ID and Secret Key. No separate Python install is required. Add credentials on the API page, review your strategy and limits, then decide whether to start. Wallet-address copy trading is not supported on Polymarket US.

How does the Polymarket referral offer work?

New Polymarket US users can join with referral code ROMANR and make a qualifying $10 deposit to unlock the current $50 trading reward. Eligibility and Polymarket terms apply, and the offer may change or expire. ROM may also receive a referral reward. View the offer ↗

Does a trading bot guarantee a profit?

No. Polybot’s signal scores are heuristics, not verified win probabilities. Its live-data practice uses simulated fills, and practice results can differ materially from exchange execution. Its live authenticated trading and profitability remain unverified. Automated trading can lose money.

Can I use a Mac or my phone?

Nova is available in your browser. The downloadable desktop apps on this page are for Windows; there are no native Mac or mobile installers listed.

Why might Windows show a publisher warning?

The installers are not yet code-signed. Verify your download and review the signing and verification information before deciding whether to install. A matching checksum confirms file integrity, not software safety.
