Helius key · optionalnot set
The keyless stream goes blind once a token leaves the bonding curve. A free Helius key lets the Whale Radar keep following its top wallets on every venue, so a proven wallet's record keeps growing after graduation. The key stays in this browser and is sent to helius-rpc.com and nowhere else; in Helius's dashboard you can restrict which sites may use it.
Remote worker · optional, for 24/7not connected
This device stops hunting when the app closes. The repo ships the same engine as a deployable service (worker/README) that never sleeps; connect yours here and a source toggle appears above to read its feed. ROM runs one — it asks for a sign-in, on the Account page, which also connects it.

Radar data is measured by YOUR app (or your worker) from its own observed stream: pump.fun bonding-curve trades, program-wide, while armed. Scores stand on settled, fully-observed round trips; grades and exits on later trades in the same stream, marked to the last trade seen when a token goes quiet and blind once it leaves the curve. The copy desk records what you type and marks it the same way. Signals are observations, not advice, nothing here executes trades, and no arrangement of these numbers makes a memecoin a good idea.
