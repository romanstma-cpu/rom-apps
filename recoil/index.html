Questions

About ROM Recoil, its settings and the download.

Is ROM Recoil free?

Yes. Download the zip, unzip it and run RomRecoil.exe on a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit). There's no account, key or installer.

Which games does it work in?

It's built for Rainbow Six Siege. It pulls whenever you fire, in any window except its own; turn on Settings → Only in Rainbow Six Siege to limit it to the game.

What do the settings mean?

Vertical pull (0–100) counters upward kick and horizontal pull counters sideways drift. Strength multiplies both; Boost raises its limit from 10 to 25. Activate when picks left click (every shot) or aim + fire (only while you hold right click). The readout at the top of Tuning shows the result in mouse counts per second.

How do I update?

ROM Recoil checks romapps.xyz when it opens, and every few hours while it runs, and shows an Update button in its title bar when a newer version is out. Download the new zip, close ROM Recoil, and run the new RomRecoil.exe. Your profiles and settings carry over.

Why does the status say “Input blocked”?

Windows refused the mouse movement, so nothing would happen. This usually means another program, such as a different game's anti-cheat, is blocking input. It clears once the movement goes through again.

Where are my profiles saved?

In %APPDATA%\RomRecoil: settings.json and one .ini file per profile in configs. Settings → Data folder opens it. To remove ROM Recoil, exit it from the tray, then delete RomRecoil.exe and that folder.

Can ROM Recoil get me banned?

It can. Ubisoft's terms don't allow third-party tools that give an advantage, and any macro can be detected. ROM does not claim it is undetectable; use it at your own risk.

Why might Windows warn about the download?

The app isn't code-signed yet. Use the published checksum and the verification steps to check the file before you open it. A matching checksum confirms file integrity, not software safety.
