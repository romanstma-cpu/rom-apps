# Customer

## Job Statement

When I want to automate a Polymarket US trading approach without watching every
market myself, I want to connect an account, understand what the automation is
doing, and keep its risk bounded, so I can participate without giving up control
of my money or confidence in what happened.

## Job Dimensions

| Dimension | Need | Current underdelivery / evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Functional | Connect safely, observe the strategy, set limits, and stop it quickly. | The prior kill switch could request exits without pausing new entries. Fixed in the public-launch update; verify with real users. |
| Emotional | Feel informed and in control before real orders can be placed. | The former live-review dialog displayed limits but did not require an explicit acknowledgement. Fixed; test whether the wording is understood. |
| Social | Feel responsible rather than reckless when using automation with money. | Practice-first guidance and precise safety copy need customer-language validation. |

## Competing Alternatives

| Alternative | Why hired | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Manual Polymarket US trading | Direct control over every order. | Requires continuous attention and disciplined execution. |
| Spreadsheet, alerts, or notes | Low-risk way to track ideas. | Does not execute or reconcile orders. |
| Another trading bot | Automation and potential speed. | May require broader permissions, opaque logic, or different market support. |
| Non-consumption | Avoids trading and automation risk. | Does not make progress on the user's intended strategy. |
